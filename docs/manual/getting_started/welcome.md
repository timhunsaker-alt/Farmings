Farmings follows real farming: animals have a sex, a breed and genes, eat what real animals eat, and grow, breed and give milk, wool,
eggs and meat on a real clock.

- Animals spawn **wild**. Lure them into a [taming corral](corral) to make them part of your herd.
- Machines run on power from **Wildcatter**.
- Food spoils unless it is kept cold.

Pick a chapter on the left, or type in the search box.
