The herd book keeps your breeding records. Craft it from a book and quill and a wheat, with no grid shape needed.

{item:farmings:herd_book} {item:minecraft:writable_book} {item:minecraft:wheat}

## Using it
- **Right-click an animal** with the book to record it. Recording the same animal again replaces its page.
- **Use it in the air** to read it as a book.
- It holds **64** animals, one page each.

## What a page shows
Each page is keyed by the animal's herd ID, the first **8** characters of its genome code. It lists:
- breed or cross, sex and birth day;
- dam and sire IDs (founders say "founder");
- calves, and whether a heifer is a freemartin;
- the eight trait scores, dressing % and marbling grade.

Use it to pick sires and dams and to spot close relatives before you [breed](breeding). The scores are explained under [genes and traits](genes).

## The stud book
Horses have their own **stud book**, crafted from a book and quill and leather. It holds up to **64** horses, with breed, colour, speed
gene, fitness, races, wins and best times, plus sire, dam and grandparents. See [horses](horses) and [the race track](race-track).
