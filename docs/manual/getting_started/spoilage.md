Farmings food goes off. Vanilla beef and milk buckets don't.

## The clock
- Shelf lives are real days played as game days.
- Most fresh food keeps **1** game day out of the cold.
- The clock starts when food leaves the cold: when it is bottled or taken at the creamery, taken by a hopper, dropped by an animal, or
  first carried.
- The **bar** under the icon shows the life left: green when fresh, through yellow, to red.
- Food in a chest outside a fridge isn't checked until you take it. Then it turns at once if its time has passed.

## In a cold room
A powered [cold room](cold-room) holds food at about **2** °C, and food in its chests and barrels lasts its fridge life:
- bottled milk **7** days, cream **3.5**, butter **45**;
- a cheddar wheel **180** days;
- raw steak and chops **4** days, bacon **7**, raw sausage **1.5**.

The parlour bulk tank and the creamery count as cold too.

## Spoiled food
Food that runs out turns into a spoiled item: a spoiled bottle, rancid butter, moldy cheese, spoiled steak, spoiled mutton, spoiled pork
or spoiled poultry. You can eat it for **1** hunger, but it gives Hunger for **30** s and Nausea for **15** s.
