## Seeds
Break grass by hand to find seeds. Which ones you get depends on the biome:
- plains: corn, alfalfa and oats;
- savanna: corn and alfalfa;
- meadow, forest and birch forest: timothy and orchardgrass;
- taiga: oats and barley.

A harvested block gives back 1-2 seeds.

## Growing
Crops grow on the **15:1** clock, with no random ticks, so a field grows the same whether you watch it or not. Each crop has 8 stages.
- **Alfalfa, timothy and orchardgrass** are perennials: cut them and they grow back. Alfalfa is ready again every **32.5** real days
  (**2.2** game days) and lasts **22** cuts.
- **Corn** is 2 blocks tall. Chop it from **125** days for silage; combine it at **130** days or later for grain.
- **Oats and barley** give grain and leave straw.

Yields are scaled up ten times, so **1 t/ha** in real life gives **1 kg** per block.

Compost, liquid digestate and bone meal all work as bone meal. See [compost](compost) and [the digester](digester).

## Harvest
Work big fields with the [field gantry](field-gantry). By hand, a ripe block drops loose hay or straw in **10 kg** items. A round bale
weighs **454 kg** and crafts into **4** vanilla hay blocks.
