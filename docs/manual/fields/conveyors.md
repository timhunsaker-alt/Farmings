Belts and augers carry crops, feed and manure between your machines.

{item:farmings:farm_belt} {item:farmings:farm_belt_drive} {item:farmings:grain_auger} {item:farmings:grain_auger_drive}

## Belt conveyor
- Lay **farm belt** cells in a line. Place a cell one block higher or lower to make a slope, at most **1** in **3**. Two slopes closer
  than **3** cells read TOO STEEP.
- Every run needs a **belt drive** on Wildcatter power: **7.5 kW** at **480 V** for each **30 m** of run.
- Items ride at **2 m/s**, so a **20**-block run takes **10 s**. You can see them on the belt.

## Grain auger
- For grain, meal and seed only. It runs straight in any direction, up to **45°**.
- It moves **2,700 bu/h** of corn, about **19 kg/s**, however long it is.
- An **auger drive** (**3.7 kW**) powers up to **9** blocks of auger. Longer runs need a second drive.

## Loading and unloading
- A run picks up what drops on its tail: a hopper, a machine's output or the gantry's headland stop.
- It delivers into whatever its head touches: the [silo](silo) blower, a grain bin, the [feed mill](feed-mill), a barn feeder, another
  belt, a hopper or a chest.
- If the head is full, the run stops and keeps its load. With no power the drive reads NO POWER.

## Recipes
- Belt: **3** leather over **3** iron makes **6**.
- Auger: iron, stick, iron makes **4**.
