Young animals grow on a curve toward their breed's mature weight, so a herd renews itself.

## Cattle
- A calf is born at **6%** of its breed's mature weight.
- At **15** months it is about **60%**: a Holstein **425** kg, a Brown Swiss **379** kg, a Jersey **297** kg.
- At **48** months it is fully grown. A grown cow stops growing; a bull keeps growing the same way.
- Mature cows weigh about **710** kg (Holstein), **470** kg (Jersey) and **487** kg (Wagyu).
- Calves need no feed. Housed heifers and bulls grow by how well they are fed; animals outside the barn grow at full rate.
- An underfed heifer just grows slower, and reaches her breeding weight later. Nothing dies of it.

## Other animals
- **Sheep:** lambs are weaned at **84** days. A ewe lamb is first bred at **65%** of mature weight.
- **Pigs:** **1.5** kg at birth, **5.7** kg at **21** days, **120** kg at **186** days.
- **Horses:** **43%** of mature weight at **6** months, **64%** at **12**, **77%** at **18**, **86%** at **24**, mature at **5** years.
