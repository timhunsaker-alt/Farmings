Only tame herd animals breed. A female breeds while she is in heat, by natural service or by a straw.

## Heat
- A cow comes into heat every **21** days, for **0.75** of a real day. Heart particles show every **3** seconds while she is in heat.
- After calving she waits **60** days before her next heat.
- Sheep, goats and horses only cycle in their season; see each animal's page.

## Natural service
- **Cattle:** a bull with his own stall in the same [barn](barn) serves any cow in heat there.
- **Sheep and goats:** a ram or buck serves in the same pen or pasture. **Horses:** a stallion in the same stable or paddock.

## Straws
- Craft **8** empty straws from a Wildcatter plastic sheet.
- Use an empty straw on a tame grown bull, ram, buck, boar, stallion or turkey tom: it becomes a semen straw holding his genes.
- Use the semen straw on a female of the same species in heat (a hen needs no heat). Any other animal refuses it.
- Bull, ram, buck and stallion straws keep forever. Boar straws keep **6** days; turkey straws go stale after **30** real minutes.

## Conception and birth
- Cattle, sheep and goats conceive at every service in heat. Pigs conceive **84%** of the time, horses **60%**.
- A player's first two calves are heifers and the third a bull; after that it's even.
- Twins come in **5.02%** of later calvings and **0.68%** of first ones.
- Stillbirths: **12.3%** for heifers, **5.4%** for cows.
- A heifer born twin to a bull is a freemartin and can't breed.
