- **Use it on an animal:** its temperature against the normal range (a dairy cow **38.0-39.3** C), what it has, its vaccines and when its
  milk and meat are clear.
- **Sneak and use it on a sick animal:** to the nearest sick pen with room. On a notifiable disease it reports it instead.
- **Use it on a housing controller:** the herd check, every animal that is sick, withheld, due a dose or in quarantine. With the
  [herd book](herd-book) in the other hand the check is written into it.

## The CMT paddle
With a reagent bottle in your inventory, the paddle reads a cow's somatic cells: N under **200,000** a mL, T, 1, 2 or 3. The
parlor's readout gives the bulk tank's count and warns **HIGH SCC** over **750,000**.
