{item:farmings:isolation_controller} {item:farmings:isolation_pen}

A controller beside **2** to **64** cells of pen floor, a feeder and a trough, with no other housing within **8** blocks.

- **Quarantine:** a wild animal tamed in a corral within **64** blocks goes here first for **30** real days (**2** game days for cattle).
  Some arrive carrying disease that quarantine keeps from the herd.
- **Hospital:** the vet kit sends a sick animal here; it leaves when it has recovered.
- Sneak and use the controller to switch modes while the pen is empty. One species at a time.

## Notifiable diseases
With the world rule on, a vet kit that finds one says to report it. A report puts every Farmings housing within **192** blocks under a
standstill: no animal in or out, no taming, no straws, until **28** real days after the last case. There is no culling.
