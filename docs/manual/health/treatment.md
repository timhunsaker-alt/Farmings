One item is one course. A treated animal wears a red leg band until its withdrawal is over.

- **Mastitis tube:** mastitis. Milk withheld **96** h, meat **4** days.
- **Long-acting injection:** shipping fever, foot rot, erysipelas, scours. Milk **10** days, meat **28** days.
- **Electrolytes:** scours, no withdrawal. **Coccidiostat:** use it on a coop controller to dose every bird.
- Withheld milk never reaches the tank: the parlor milks the cow into a **waste milk can** beside it. With no can she is not called.
- An animal killed during its meat withdrawal drops no meat.
- **Teat dip:** load it into the parlor controller; one bottle dips **50** cows.
