{item:farmings:vaccine_respiratory} {item:farmings:vaccine_cdt} {item:farmings:vaccine_erysipelas} {item:farmings:vaccine_mareks} {item:farmings:vaccine_strangles}

Vaccines keep only in the [cold room](cold-room): out of it they spoil in a game day.

- **Respiratory** (cattle): halves shipping fever. **CDT** (sheep, goats): **2** doses **4** weeks apart, ewes **4** weeks before lambing.
- **Erysipelas** (pigs), **strangles** (horses): two doses, then yearly. **Marek's**: load it into an [incubator](hatchery); each chick
  that hatches is vaccinated.
- Vaccinate a cow (respiratory) or a ewe or doe (CDT) in the **6** weeks before she gives birth: her young get antibodies in the first
  milk, and half the risk of scours.
- A booster falls due a year after the last dose; the herd check lists it.

## The footbath
{item:farmings:footbath} {item:farmings:zinc_sulfate}

Set it in a lane or a gate path, fill it with water and charge it with **10%** zinc sulfate (**100** passes a bag). Every animal that walks
through is treated for foot rot and protected **14** real days. Each one carries **1** L out on its feet: top the bath up before it falls
under **60** L (a water pump's pipe keeps it full). A pasture a lame sheep grazed stays infective **7** real days.
