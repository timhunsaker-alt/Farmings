Four breeds: **Pekin**, **Khaki Campbell**, **Mallard** and **Muscovy**. Wild ducks spawn near water in swamps, rivers and
mangrove swamps. They follow grain, and you tame them in a [taming corral](corral). Ducks swim, and go out on the run even in rain.

Ducks run at the bird clock, **1 game day = 3 real days** ([time scales](time-scales)).

## Laying and breeding
- First egg at **26 weeks**.
- Eggs a year: Pekin **140**, Khaki Campbell **250**, Mallard **35**, Muscovy **90**.
- One drake serves up to **5** ducks; eggs stay fertile for **21** real days.
- Eggs hatch after **28** days; Muscovy eggs take **35**. Muscovies are good sitters; Pekins seldom sit.

## The pond
Ducks need **1** water block per **4** ducks, in or touching the [coop](coop)'s run. A duck short of water loses **5%** of its
health a game day, but never dies of it.

## Keeping
- In the coop, **1** floor block holds **2** ducks, and each needs **2** run blocks.
- A killed duck drops **1** down item as well as meat.
