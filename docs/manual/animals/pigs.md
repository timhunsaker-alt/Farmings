Three breeds: **Large White**, **Duroc** and **Berkshire**. Wild pigs replace vanilla pigs, one boar to a group. They follow grain and are
tamed on grain in a [corral](corral). House them in a [pig barn](pig-barn).

## Breeding
- A gilt is first bred at **210** days and **136** kg. Heat comes every **21** days.
- Gestation is **114** days. Litters: Large White **11.6** at the first, **13.7** later; Duroc **10.4**; Berkshire **9.2**.
- A service conceives **84%** of the time. Boar [straws](breeding) keep **6** days.
- Piglets weigh **1.5** kg at birth and are weaned at **21** days. They need a heat lamp until then.

## Growing and feeding
- A pig reaches **120** kg at **186** days.
- Pigs eat grain, meal and rations from the feeder: a finisher about **2.2** kg a day, a nursing sow **6.5**.
- Out on pasture, pigs root up grass for part of their feed. Indoors they never change blocks.

## Pork
A **120** kg pig gives **2** hams, **24** chops, **5** bellies, **10** shoulders and **12** fat. Piglets under **30** kg give chops only.
Turn them into bacon, country ham, lard and sausage in the [curing room](curing-room).
