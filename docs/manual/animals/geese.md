Three breeds: **Embden**, **Toulouse** and **Chinese**. Embden and Toulouse spawn wild by rivers and swamps, Chinese geese on the
plains. Tame them in a [taming corral](corral) with grain. Geese run at the bird clock ([time scales](time-scales)).

## Laying
Geese lay in a season, from their first year on. A new world starts on **1** October, so the first eggs come about **46** game
days in.
- Embden and Toulouse: **15 Feb - 15 Jun**. Chinese: **1 Feb - 30 Jun**.
- Eggs a year: Embden **40**, Toulouse **27.5**, Chinese **55**. Eggs weigh **170 g** (Chinese **120 g**).
- One gander serves **4** geese. Eggs hatch after **30 days**.

## Grazing
On a grassy run a goose gets **two-thirds** of its feed from grass, so it wears a run much faster than a hen. A goose needs
**320 g** a day in all; the grain feeder tops up the rest. In the [coop](coop), **1** floor block holds **1** goose.

## Guard geese
A tame goose honks and charges a hostile mob, or a player who isn't the coop's owner, on its run or within **4 blocks** of it. It
bites for **1 damage** a second. Chinese geese guard at twice the range.
