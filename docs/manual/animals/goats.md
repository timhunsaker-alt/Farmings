Three breeds: **Saanen** and **Nubian** for milk, **Boer** for meat. Wild goats replace vanilla goats in snowy slopes, jagged peaks and
frozen peaks, in groups of 2-4. Wild goats ram; tame ones don't. Tame them in a [corral](corral) on hay or wheat, in **7** real days.

## Breeding
- A doeling is first bred at **7** months and **60%** of mature weight. Gestation is **150** days.
- Seasons: Saanen **1** Sep to **28** Feb, Nubian **1** Aug to **31** Mar, Boer all year.
- Careful with polled goats: a doe with two polled genes is intersex and can't breed, and half of such bucks are sterile. The
  [herd book](herd-book) shows the genes.
- Bucks fill [straws](breeding) as bulls and rams do.

## Milk
- A lactation is **284** days. Saanen give **1,154** kg at **3.2%** fat; Nubian **832** kg at **4.6%**.
- Does are milked in the [parlour's](parlour) small stalls into a goat tank. The [creamery](creamery) bottles goat milk and makes
  chèvre.

## Housing and meat
- Goats live in the [sheep shed](sheep-shed), in their own pen run: one goat to **2** pen blocks.
- A goat on pasture beside leaves browses them, so it wears the grass half as fast.
- Goats under **9** months give **kid** chops, older ones **chevon**.
