Six breeds: **Holstein**, **Jersey**, **Brown Swiss** and **Gyr** for milk, **Angus** and **Wagyu** for beef. They spawn wild, with the
breed matched to the biome. Lead them with wheat or a hay block and tame them in a [corral](corral).

## Life
- A heifer is first bred at **15** months, once she weighs **55%** of her breed's mature weight.
- Cows come into heat every **21** days. Gestation is **283** days, about **18.9** game days.
- A player's first two calves are heifers, the third a bull, then it's even.
- Right-click with an empty hand to read an animal. Shears on a bull calf make him a steer.

## Feed and milk
- Cows eat hay in the [barn](barn). A **100** kg hay block holds **85** kg of dry matter; a milking cow eats about a quarter of a block a
  game day.
- Water: **6** L per kg of dry matter, plus **1** L per litre of milk.
- Dairy cows are milked once a game day in the [parlour](parlour). A lactation is **305** days, then **60** days dry. A first lactation
  gives **15%** less. Beef cows are never milked.
- Underfed cows give less milk and grow slower, but never die of it.

## Beef
Steaks are graded **Select**, **Choice** or **Prime** by marbling. A **550** kg Angus gives **13** beef and **3** Choice steaks; a **487** kg
Wagyu gives **10** beef and **4** Prime steaks. See [meat and food](meat).
