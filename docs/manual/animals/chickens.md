Four breeds: **Leghorn**, **Rhode Island Red**, **Plymouth Rock** and **Silkie**. Wild chickens replace vanilla ones in plains,
hills, badlands and jungles. They follow grain, and you tame them in a [taming corral](corral) with grain in the bait trough.

Birds run on their own clock: **1 game day = 3 real days**, and a hen lays one real day's eggs each game day. See
[time scales](time-scales).

## Laying
- First egg at **20 weeks** (the Leghorn at **143 days**).
- Eggs a year: Leghorn **235**, Rhode Island Red **250**, Plymouth Rock about **200**, Silkie about **100**.
- A hen retires after **3** laying years.
- Silkies go broody easily and hatch eggs for you; Leghorns rarely do.

## Breeding
- One rooster serves up to **10** hens. A hen lays fertile eggs for **28** real days after mating.
- Eggs hatch after **21** days, under a broody hen or in an [incubator](hatchery).

## Keeping
- One grain item is **100 g** of feed, about one item a hen each game day.
- In the [coop](coop), **1** floor block holds **3** chickens, with a nest box for every **4** hens.
