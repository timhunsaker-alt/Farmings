Two breeds: the **Coturnix** (Japanese quail), wild in savannas, and the **Northern Bobwhite**, wild in plains. Tame them in a
[taming corral](corral) with grain.

Quail run at the bird clock, **1 game day = 3 real days** ([time scales](time-scales)).

## Laying
- Coturnix: first egg at **7 weeks**, **250** eggs a year. Hens rarely go broody.
- Bobwhite: first egg at about **22 weeks**, **125** eggs a year. Hens are broody.

## Breeding
- One cock serves up to **3** hens; eggs stay fertile for **11** real days.
- The two breeds don't cross: a cock serves only hens of his own kind.
- Eggs hatch after **17** days (Coturnix) or **23.5** (Bobwhite).

## Keeping
- A quail eats about **16 g** of feed a day.
- In the [coop](coop), **1** floor block holds **16** quail, with a nest box for every **8** hens. Quail are ground birds and
  don't use perches.
- A killed quail always drops at least one quail item.
