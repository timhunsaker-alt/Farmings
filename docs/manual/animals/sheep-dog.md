A working Border Collie that gathers and drives your flock.

## Taming
- Dogs spawn wild in pairs in plains, meadows and windswept hills.
- A wild dog keeps **6** blocks from empty hands, and follows raw meat.
- In a [corral](corral) it settles on raw meat in the bait trough: one meat item (**0.5** kg) a day. The trough holds **16** meat items.
- After **7** real days settled it is yours.
- Dogs don't breed, and don't age past adult.

## The whistle
Craft a **shepherd's whistle** from **2** iron nuggets and **1** string.

{item:farmings:shepherds_whistle}

- **Sneak-use on a block** to set the target: a sheep shed controller sends the flock to its pen, a pasture gate to the pasture, any
  other block to that spot.
- **Sneak-use in the air** to pick a command.
- **Use in the air** to blow it. Your tame dogs within **64** blocks hear it.

## Commands
- **Come bye:** the dog runs round every tame sheep within **32** blocks and brings them to you.
- **Walk up:** it drives them to the target.
- **Lie down:** it stops, and the sheep stand.
- **That'll do:** it stops working and comes back to heel.

Only tame sheep are herded. A shed's sheep driven into its pen go back under the [shed's](sheep-shed) rules.
