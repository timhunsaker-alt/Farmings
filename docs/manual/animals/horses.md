Three breeds: **Thoroughbred**, **Quarter Horse** and **Arabian**, bred for racing. Wild horses replace vanilla horses in plains and
savanna. A wild horse throws its rider: tame it in a [corral](corral) on hay, in **7** real days. Then saddle it and ride it as a vanilla
horse. House it in a [stable](stable).

## Breeding
- Mares come into heat every **21** days, from **15** Apr to **31** Oct.
- A service conceives **60%** of the time. Stallion [straws](breeding) keep, as bull straws do.
- Gestation is **340** days, for one foal. Foals are weaned at **150** days.
- Horses under **2** years can't be ridden.

## Speed
- **The speed gene (MSTN):** C/C horses are sprinters, best at about **1,250** m; C/T at **1,830** m; T/T horses are stayers, best at
  **2,110** m.
- **SPEED** and **STAMINA** traits fine-tune the pace and how it holds over distance.
- **Age:** a horse peaks at **4.45** years.
- **Fitness:** galloping builds it. An unfit horse runs **5%** slower. A rested horse keeps its fitness **42** real days, then loses it.
- Races and riding are real time. Race at [the race track](race-track).

Horses aren't kept for meat: one that dies drops its saddle and nothing else.
