Three breeds: **Broad Breasted White**, **Bronze** and **Bourbon Red**. Bronze and Bourbon Red spawn wild in forests, taiga and
hills; tame them in a [taming corral](corral) with grain. The Broad Breasted White never spawns wild.

Turkeys run at the bird clock, **1 game day = 3 real days** ([time scales](time-scales)).

## Laying
- First egg at **31.5** weeks. A hen lays for **28 weeks**, then rests until a year from her first egg.
- Eggs a year: Broad Breasted White **110**, Bronze about **75**, Bourbon Red **45**.

## Breeding
- One tom serves up to **10** hens; her eggs stay fertile for **21** real days.
- Eggs hatch after **28** days.
- A Broad Breasted White tom can't mate. Collect his semen with an empty straw and use it on a turkey hen. The straw spoils
  **30 real minutes** after collection, so use it at once. See [breeding](breeding).

## Keeping
- In the [coop](coop), **1** floor block holds **1** turkey, and each needs **2** run blocks.
- A Bronze tom of **16.6 kg** gives **26** turkey items.
