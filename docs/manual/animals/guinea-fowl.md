The **Helmeted** guinea fowl comes in pearl, lavender or white, set by its genes. Wild guinea fowl spawn in savannas; tame them
in a [taming corral](corral) with grain. They run at the bird clock ([time scales](time-scales)).

## Laying
- First egg at **28 weeks**. They lay in season, **1 Apr - 31 Oct**.
- About **100** eggs a year, at **40 g** each.
- A guinea hen goes broody only once her nest holds **30** eggs.
- One male serves **5** hens. Eggs hatch after **27 days**.

## The alarm call
A tame guinea fowl that sees a hostile mob within **16 blocks** calls out, at most once every **10 s**. Every bird of its
[coop](coop) out on the run goes inside for a minute, and the coop readout shows `ALARM` and the mob. Wild guinea fowl call too,
and flee.

## Keeping
- In the coop, **1** floor block holds **4** guinea fowl, and they roost on perches.
- On a grassy run they find **20%** of their feed themselves.
