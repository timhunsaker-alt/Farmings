Turn [pork](pigs) into country ham, bacon, lard and sausage. These are separate blocks; the curing rack needs a cold room.

## Salt and cure
- Craft **salt** from a water bucket and coal. The bucket comes back.
- **4** salt and **1** sugar make **5** cure.

## The curing rack and smokehouse
- The rack holds up to **4** pieces of fresh ham or pork belly, each with its cure.
- It must stand in a powered [cold room](cold-room). Above **4.5** °C the cure stops, and a piece warm for **1** real day spoils.
- A **smokehouse** beside the rack smokes each piece when its turn comes. One log, charcoal or coal burns for **2** game hours.
- **Country ham:** cure **2** days per lb, rest **14** days, smoke **2** days, then age **90** days on the rack, no cold needed. It ends at
  **77.5%** of its fresh weight and keeps **365** days even out of the cold. Slice it at a crafting table.
- **Bacon:** cure **7** days, then hot smoke **3** hours.
- Times run 15:1: a **60**-day cure takes **4** game days.

## Lard and sausage
- A **lard kettle** over a lit campfire or fire renders pork fat: **0.75** kg of lard per kg of fat, **3** real hours a batch.
- A hand-cranked **meat grinder**: **3** pork shoulders, **2** pork fat and **1** salt give **32** sausage links. Cook them in a furnace,
  smoker or campfire.
