Meat drops from an animal when it dies. Nothing is sold: food is for eating and for making other food.

## Beef
- The carcass is **63%** of live weight for beef breeds, **58%** for dairy. One item stands for **10** kg.
- Steaks are graded by marbling: **Select**, **Choice** or **Prime**. Wagyu give more steaks.
- The rest drops as vanilla beef, plus **1-3** leather.
- Cooked Select steak is vanilla steak (**8** hunger); Choice gives **9**, Prime **10**.

## Lamb, mutton, kid and chevon
- Sheep under a year give lamb chops, older ones mutton chops. One item per **2.5** kg of meat.
- Goats under **9** months give kid chops, older ones chevon chops.

## Pork
Pigs dress out at **70%**, giving hams, chops, bellies, shoulders and fat. See [pigs](pigs) and the [curing room](curing-room).

## Poultry and eggs
See the bird pages, such as [chickens](chickens), and [eggs](eggs).

## Cooking and keeping
- Cook raw meat in a furnace (**200** ticks), smoker (**100**) or campfire (**600**).
- Raw steak keeps **4** days in a [cold room](cold-room) and **1** day out. Cooked steak keeps **3.5** days cold.

Horses are not kept for meat.
