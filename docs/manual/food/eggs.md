Every Farmings egg carries its weight, shell colour, the day it was laid, and whether it is fertile. Eggs of the same size and
grade stack well; ungraded eggs stack poorly, so pack them in cartons.

## The egg grading table
A hand-worked table with no power. Put chicken eggs in by hand or hopper; it packs **12** eggs of one size and grade into an egg
carton. A carton opens back into its eggs. Turkey, duck, quail, goose and guinea eggs are packed **12** to a carton by species,
with no size.

**Sizes**, by the egg's least weight:
- Jumbo **70.9 g**, Extra Large **63.8 g**, Large **56.7 g**
- Medium **49.6 g**, Small **42.5 g**, Peewee **35.4 g**

**Grades**, by age:
- **AA:** laid less than **7** days ago, and clean.
- **A:** less than **21** days.
- **B:** older, or a dirty floor egg.

## Keeping eggs
- In a powered [cold room](cold-room) an egg keeps **35 days**; out of the cold, **7 days**.
- A spoiled egg becomes a **rotten egg**. It isn't food, but you can throw it: a hit gives Nausea for **5 seconds**.
- Any Farmings egg works in cake and pumpkin pie, and fries into a fried egg.
