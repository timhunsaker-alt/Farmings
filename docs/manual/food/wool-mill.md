The wool mill is a line of separate machines on Wildcatter power. Each takes wool in at the top or sides and gives its output below,
so hoppers can chain them. Right-click with an empty hand to take the output or read the machine. A batch takes **5** seconds.

## The machines
- **Scouring bowl** (**3** kW): fleece, skirtings, bales or loose wool, plus water, become clean wool and lanolin. It uses **15** L of
  water per kg and holds **1,000** L, filled by bucket or pipe.
- **Carding machine** (**1.5** kW): clean wool to sliver.
- **Spinning frame** (**1** kW): sliver to yarn in the wool's natural colour, **100** g a skein. Finer wool gives more yarn.
- **Power loom** (**0.75** kW): **4** yarn of one colour make **1** wool cloth.
- **Felting table** (**0.5** kW): **0.25** kg of clean wool makes **1** felt.

## What wool is for
- **Wool blocks:** **4** yarn of one colour make a vanilla wool block. White yarn plus a vanilla dye gives dyed yarn.
- **Clothing:** a knitted cap, sweater, trousers and socks from wool cloth, in the armour shapes. They protect like leather, and any one
  piece keeps powder snow's freezing off.
- **Felt** insulates [cold room](cold-room) panels: **3** iron, **3** felt and **3** iron make **4**.
- **Udder balm:** **2** lanolin make one. It heals a hurt cow or sheep by **4** health.
